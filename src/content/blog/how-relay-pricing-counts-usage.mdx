OpenE2EE Signal Protocol Relay plans include monthly active accounts, destination-device deliveries, attachment uploads, and live encrypted storage.
Each meter measures a different part of the service. The [pricing table](/pricing) lists the current allowances and rates.

## The base price includes active accounts

Free includes 100 monthly active accounts. Starter includes 1,000, Growth includes 5,000, and Business includes 25,000.
The monthly base prices are $0, $99, $499, and $999.

Starter with 1,000 active accounts costs $99 when the other meters remain within their allowances.
With accepted MAU overage, Starter with 2,000 active accounts costs $149 under the same condition.
Each additional active account costs $0.05 on Starter, $0.03 on Growth, or $0.02 on Business.
Additional active accounts do not increase the other allowances.

## One message can use several delivery units

One accepted encrypted envelope or shared-body reference uses one delivery unit for each destination device.
One stored Relay delivery receipt for one sender device also uses one delivery unit.
A send to two destination devices uses two units. While Relay stores the message, an exact retry with the same stable operation identifier does not charge again.

Included delivery units are 100,000 on Free, 500,000 on Starter, 2.5 million on Growth, and 12.5 million on Business.
Paid excess delivery costs $200, $100, or $60 per million units, respectively.

## Uploads and storage are separate

An attachment upload counts when Relay accepts its upload authorization. Exact retries do not count again.
Free includes 20,000 authorizations, Starter 100,000, Growth 500,000, and Business 2.5 million. There is no paid upload overage.
These fixed allowances do not require a percentage of messages with attachments.

Live encrypted storage includes customer ciphertext and attachment bytes. It excludes internal indexes and bookkeeping.
The included capacities are 1 GB, 10 GB, 50 GB, and 250 GB.

Paid excess storage costs $1.00, $0.75, or $0.55 per exact GB-month on Starter, Growth, or Business.
One GB is 1,000,000,000 bytes. One GB-month uses the actual UTC calendar month.
One excess GB held for all of February or March costs the same listed monthly rate.
Retention changes storage usage. A large upload that remains for a month consumes more byte-time than one removed promptly.

## Compare the complete bill

At 20 million delivery units, Growth costs $2,249 and Business costs $1,449 when the other meters fit.
Business still has 7.5 million excess delivery units in this example. Ignoring them would overstate the upgrade savings.
Check MAU, storage, and the nonchargeable upload limit before changing plans.

Overage requires explicit acceptance and a spend limit. A stored payment method does not authorize it.
Plan enforcement follows reported usage, so it does not promise an instantaneous stop at the first excess unit.
Mailbox pull and acknowledgement remain separate from billing summaries.

Every plan contains the same protocol features. Each project also gets a separate Sandbox environment, with no card required.
The SDK is available under MIT or Apache-2.0. You do not need to buy an SDK license.
